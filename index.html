Our Story

We Didn't Open a Restaurant. We Started a Movement.

BurgerRush was born in 2015 from one rebellious idea: fast food doesn't have to be boring. We were sick of soggy buns, mystery meat, and unmemorable flavors. So we rebuilt the whole thing from scratch.

Today, our team of passionate chefs smashes, seasons, and serves over 50,000 orders a month — all made fresh, always consistent, and with an obsessive focus on flavor.

  • Never frozen – always fresh-made patties
  • Premium locally-sourced ingredients
  • Award-winning recipes since 2017
